Overview

Toasts confirm; the center keeps what matters.

Built from: toast, alert, list.

When to use

  • After an action.
  • When something happens in the background.

Structure and live example

Idea curated
Toast.

<div class="toast toast--success"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><circle cx="12" cy="12" r="9"/><path d="m8 12 3 3 5-6"/></svg><div class="toast__body">Idea curated</div></div>

States

Stacked toasts show newest nearest the edge, at most three.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts are full width on phones.

Accessibility

  • role="status" for toasts; role="alert" for errors.
  • Pause on hover and focus.

Content

  • Past tense, one sentence.
