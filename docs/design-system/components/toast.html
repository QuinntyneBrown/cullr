Toast

A brief message that confirms something that just happened.

Overview

Toasts confirm and never block. They disappear after 6 seconds (30 seconds if they have an action) and pause while hovered or focused. Errors that need action are alerts.

Anatomy

  1. IconStatus.
  2. TitleWhat happened.
  3. BodyOptional detail or Undo.
  4. Close44px target.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Live region: role="status" with aria-live="polite"; errors role="alert".

Keyboard

Keyboard
KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from interactive content in DOM order.
EscDismisses the focused toast.

Focus

Toasts never take focus; their buttons are reachable in DOM order.

Labelling

Close is Dismiss.

Contrast

Text on toast

Motion

Slides and fades over --duration-base; reduced motion shows and hides instantly.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px where the control stands alone; neighbouring targets are 8px apart.

Content

  • Past tense: Idea archived.
  • One short sentence of detail.
  • Offer Undo instead of a confirmation.
