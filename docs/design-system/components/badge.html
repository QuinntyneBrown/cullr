Badge

Overview

Badges label; they are not buttons. Always include text; colour only reinforces.

Anatomy

  1. DotStatus marker.
  2. LabelOne or two words.

Variants

NeutralToronto & GTACuratedNeeds critiqueArchived

Sizes

One size.

States

Static; badges do not have hover or focus states.

Responsive behaviour

  • Wrap in a cluster; never truncate.
