Overview

Alerts stay until resolved or dismissed. A .banner is a full-width alert at the top of the page for offline and session states.

Anatomy

  1. IconStatus.
  2. TitleWhat happened.
  3. BodyWhat to do.
  4. Actions (optional).alert__actions.

Sizes

Full width of container.

States

Static; the dismissible variant adds a close button with hover and focus like an icon button.

Responsive behaviour

  • Actions wrap beneath the text below 768px.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert" for errors (assertive), role="status" for the rest.

Keyboard

Keyboard
KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from interactive content in DOM order.

Focus

Focus is not moved; for form errors focus the summary.

Labelling

Icon is decorative; title carries meaning, so colour is never alone.

Contrast

Info
Success
Warning
Danger

Motion

Fades in over --duration-base.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px where the control stands alone; neighbouring targets are 8px apart.

Content

  • Title: what happened.
  • Body: what to do next.
