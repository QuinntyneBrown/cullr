Menu

A short list of actions opened from a button.

Overview

A menu holds up to about seven related actions that would clutter the page, such as the row actions on an idea. It opens from a button and closes on choice, Escape or outside click. Use a select to choose a value.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  2. Panel.menu, raised surface, level-2 shadow.
  3. Item.menu__item, 40px minimum height.
  4. DividerSeparates the destructive group.

Variants

Standard item and destructive item (menu__item--danger).

Responsive behaviour

  • On phones the menu anchors to the trigger and never exceeds 90vw.
  • Menus with many items become a dialog sheet below 768px.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA menu button: trigger with aria-haspopup="menu", panel role="menu", items role="menuitem".

Keyboard

Keyboard
KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓Opens the menu and focuses the first item.
↑ / ↓Moves between items; wraps.
Home / EndFirst / last item.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses the menu.

Focus

Focus moves into the menu on open and returns to the trigger on close. The focused item is highlighted with fill and ring.

Labelling

Trigger has a name that says what the menu is for (More actions for Porch Light). Items are verbs.

Contrast

Item text on raised surface
Danger item

Motion

No motion beyond the shared hover and focus transitions, which use --duration-fast and are removed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px where the control stands alone; neighbouring targets are 8px apart.

Content

  • Verb first: Edit, Archive.
  • Destructive items last and in danger colour, after a divider.
