Dialog

A modal panel that asks for one decision or a short form. Bottom sheet on phones.

Overview

Use a dialog when the task must be finished or cancelled before continuing: capture, edit, archive, delete, export. Not for messages; those are toasts or alerts.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop.backdrop, dims and inerts the page.
  2. PanelSurface, --radius-xl, level-3 shadow.
  3. HeaderOptional icon + title (h2).
  4. BodyContent or form.
  5. FooterActions; primary last.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA dialog: role="dialog" (or alertdialog for destructive), aria-modal="true", labelled and described.

Keyboard

Keyboard
KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles within the dialog.
EscCloses (unless busy).
EnterActivates the focused button.

Focus

On open, focus the first field, or the safest button in a confirmation. On close, return to the trigger. The page behind is inert.

Labelling

aria-labelledby the title, aria-describedby the body copy. Failures use role="alert".

Contrast

Body on panel
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Backdrop fades and the panel scales or slides in over --duration-base; reduced motion removes both.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px where the control stands alone; neighbouring targets are 8px apart.

Content

  • Title is a question or a verb phrase.
  • Primary button repeats the verb: Archive idea.
  • Say what happens next and whether it can be undone.
