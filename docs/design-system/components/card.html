Overview

Cards hold one idea, one stat or one form. They are not nested. Interactive cards navigate as a whole via a single link in the title.

Anatomy

  1. Container--radius-lg, 1px border, --space-6 padding (--space-4 on phones).
  2. TitleHeading element at the correct level.
  3. BodyText and metadata.
  4. Footer (optional)Actions, .card__footer.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A generic container. An interactive card keeps one link (the title) whose pseudo-element covers the card.

Keyboard

Keyboard
KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from interactive content in DOM order.
EnterFollows the title link.

Focus

Ring on the card container via :focus-within.

Labelling

The title is the link text; no aria-label needed.

Contrast

Body on surface
Border
Focus ring on surface

Motion

No motion beyond the shared hover and focus transitions, which use --duration-fast and are removed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Targets are at least 24px and 44px where the control stands alone; neighbouring targets are 8px apart.

Content

  • Titles are names, not sentences.
  • Three lines of body at most.
