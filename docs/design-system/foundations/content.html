Content

Plain, warm and specific. Sentence case everywhere, verbs on buttons, and every error says what happened and what to do next.

Overview

Cullr talks like a sharp colleague who respects the owner's time. It is brief, never cute, and never blames. The owner makes the ideas; Cullr only helps decide which to keep.

Voice and casing

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and menu items. Proper nouns keep their capitals.
  • Say idea, curate, archive, critique. Do not say item, entry or post.
  • Buttons start with a verb: Save changes, Archive idea, Restore idea.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, no “Oops”.

Dates, numbers and units

  • Dates read as Tue 14 Oct within the year and 14 Oct 2025 otherwise. Today and Yesterday are used within two days.
  • Counts are numerals: 3 ideas. Use a thin non-breaking space between number and unit.
  • Lists of 1000 or more use a comma: 1,204.
