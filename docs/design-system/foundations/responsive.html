Foundation

Responsive

Designed at 360px first and enhanced at 768 and 1024. Nothing scrolls sideways at 320px or at 200% zoom.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableExtracted 2026-10-14
  • Overview
  • Rules by breakpoint
  • Testing matrix

Overview

Write base styles for the phone and add min-width media queries. Use rem units so text zoom works, and use 100dvh for full-height shells.

Testing matrix

  • Widths: 360, 768 and 1280 px, in light and dark.
  • Zoom: 200% and 400% text zoom with no loss of content (WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10).
  • Orientation: portrait and landscape; nothing is locked (1.3.4).
  • Input: touch with 44px targets, and keyboard only.
