Foundation

Layout

Mobile first: 4 columns on phones, 8 on tablets, 12 on desktop, with gutters and page margins that grow at each step.

Overview

Pages are built from four templates: Shell (top bar + sidebar on desktop), Focused (one narrow column, such as a form), Auth (centred card) and Error (centred message). All use the grid below.

Page templates

  • Shell: sticky .topbar, .sidebar from 1024px, content in .container.
  • Focused: .container--narrow (32rem) for forms and settings.
  • Auth: .auth-layout with a single .auth-card.
  • Error: .empty centred in the container, always with a way out.
