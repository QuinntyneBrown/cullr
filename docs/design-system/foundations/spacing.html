Spacing

A 4px scale. Related things sit close, unrelated things sit far; control heights and touch targets are tokens too.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableExtracted 2026-10-14
  • Overview
  • Scale

Overview

Every gap, padding and margin is a --space-* token on a 4px grid. The mocks contained off-grid values (see the README drift log); they were snapped to the nearest step.

Inside and between

  • Inside a control or card: --space-3 to --space-6. Cards use --space-6 on desktop and --space-4 on mobile.
  • Between related items (label and field): --space-1 or --space-2.
  • Between fields in a form or items in a stack: --space-4.
  • Between sections of a page: --space-8 to --space-12.
  • Touch targets are at least --target-min (24px, WCAG 2.5.8) and comfortably --target-comfortable (44px).
